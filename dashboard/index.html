What this board says

Every row is a protocol whose DefiLlama record shows no token yet and that is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades. The implied value is this model's own claim about what the protocol's future token would be worth if its record stayed as it is — it is not a prediction that a token comes, and it is not make.fun's claim. The watchlist (TVL at or above the floor) is the floor of the board, never its ceiling: the early rows below it are the ones the radar exists to find first.

The model, in one line. Implied value = TVL × base multiple × growth × maturity × category × chain surface. The base multiple rises with the log of TVL; growth is the seven-day TVL change, clamped; maturity rewards protocols that have run longer without a token, with a haircut for the very young; category and chain weights come from the snapshot's own fields, never from a list of names.
